Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026 · Corridor · thomas@corridor.systems

This policy explains how Corridor collects, uses, and protects personal data in connection with corridor.systems and the services we provide.

Who we are

Corridor is a GTM advisory and software service operated by Thomas Dratler, based in Tel Aviv, Israel. You can reach us at thomas@corridor.systems.

What data we collect

When you submit the discovery call request form on this website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your work email address
  • Your company name and funding stage
  • Your target market (corridor direction)

We use a cookie consent banner to control optional visitor-identification tracking. Only with your consent do we load the RB2B tracking tool described under "Visitor identification" below; if you decline, no tracking script is loaded. We do not use cookies for advertising.

How we use your data

The information you submit is used solely to:

  • Respond to your discovery call request
  • Schedule and conduct a call about Corridor services
  • Follow up on our commercial conversation

We do not sell your data, share it with third parties for marketing purposes, or use it for automated profiling.

B2B prospecting data

As part of our GTM advisory work, Corridor processes publicly available professional information (names, job titles, company names, LinkedIn profiles) for B2B prospecting purposes. This data is sourced from professional networks and third-party enrichment tools. It is used exclusively for direct outreach relevant to the professional role of the individual contacted.

If you have received outreach from Corridor and wish to be removed from our contact list, email thomas@corridor.systems and we will remove your data within 5 business days.

Visitor identification

With your consent (collected via the cookie banner on this website), we use a visitor-identification tool to recognise B2B visitors:

  • Provider: RB2B (Retention.com)
  • Purpose: identifying B2B visitors through LinkedIn matching
  • Data processed: IP address, cookies, device ID, and LinkedIn profile where a match is found
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest (B2B)
  • Opt-out: https://app.retention.com/optout

RB2B is loaded only after you accept tracking in the cookie banner. If you decline, the script is never loaded. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing the rb2b_consent cookie or using the opt-out link above.

Data storage and security

Form submissions are sent directly to thomas@corridor.systems and are not stored in a database on this website. Prospect data used in our advisory operations is stored in a secure cloud database hosted in the EU (Ireland) by Supabase, which is compliant with GDPR data residency requirements.

Your rights under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data for direct marketing
  • Request restriction of processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at thomas@corridor.systems. We will respond within 30 days.

Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained for the duration of our commercial relationship and deleted upon request. Prospecting data is reviewed and cleaned on a regular basis; individuals who have not responded to outreach within 12 months are removed.

Third-party services

This website is hosted on Vercel (USA, with EU edge nodes). The live demo feature uses an AI content generation service to generate content. No personal data is sent to that service. Google Fonts is loaded for typography. With your consent, RB2B (Retention.com) is loaded for B2B visitor identification (see "Visitor identification" above). No other third-party services are used on this website.

For our GTM System services, we use a number of sub-processors. A categorised list of these sub-processors, including their data locations and transfer mechanisms, is available at corridor.systems/legal/sub-processors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the website after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
