Module 03 · CMO IA
Eleven specialists. Always on.
Eight marketing roles and three sales agents, each configured for a specific function. Ask anything, they respond as senior operators, not chatbots.
CMO Orchestrator - Coordinates your full GTM strategy. Weekly planning, brand positioning, channel mix, and cross-agent priorities. Start here when you need to think at the CMO level.
C
CMO Orchestrator here. Tell me what is on your plate this week - campaigns, launches, pipeline pressure, or positioning questions - and I will help you prioritise and coordinate across channels.
Positioning Analyst - Sharpens your value proposition, messaging hierarchy, and differentiation for specific markets and buyer personas.
C
Positioning Analyst here. Strong positioning is the difference between a 20-minute discovery call and a closed deal. What market are you entering, and what narrative are you leading with?
Content Strategist - Builds your thought leadership calendar, identifies topics that attract your ICP, and designs the content mix across formats and channels.
C
Content Strategist here. I build content that generates inbound from your ICP, not vanity engagement. What topic or trend do you want to own this week?
Copywriter - Writes LinkedIn posts, email sequences, landing page copy, and ad headlines calibrated to your voice and your buyer's language. Drafts on demand, edits on feedback.
C
Copywriter here. Give me a topic, a signal, or a brief and I will write it. LinkedIn post, cold email, landing page headline - tell me the format and the audience.
Repurposing Engine - Turns one piece of content into ten. Paste a post, article, or interview and get LinkedIn variants, email hooks, short-form clips, and slide ideas without losing the original voice.
C
Repurposing Engine here. Paste a piece of content and tell me what formats you need. I will extract the core idea and rebuild it for each channel without making it feel recycled.
SEO & AEO Expert - Optimises your content for search engines and AI-generated answers. Identifies queries your ICP is asking, structures content to rank, and adapts copy for zero-click and featured-snippet formats.
C
SEO and AEO here. I work on two fronts: traditional search engines and AI engines. Tell me what you want to rank for or be cited for, and I will show you how to structure it.
Distribution Manager - Selects the right channels, formats, and posting schedule for each piece of content based on your ICP's actual consumption habits. Gets content in front of the right people without wasted reach.
C
Distribution Manager here. Good content with poor distribution is just notes. Tell me what you are publishing and who needs to see it - I will build the channel and timing plan.
Performance Analyst - Reviews your content and campaigns against a fixed pre-publication checklist. Flags what will underperform before it goes out, and surfaces what is working in retrospect.
C
Performance Analyst here. Paste a draft before publishing and I will run it against the checklist. Or share results from a recent campaign and I will tell you what to change next time.
Competitive Intel - Maps your competitive landscape, identifies positioning gaps, and helps you build differentiated messaging against specific competitors.
C
Competitive Intel here. Tell me who you are up against - I will map their positioning, identify the gaps they are not covering, and show you where you win.
Prospect Agent - Deep-dives on any specific prospect: company context, stakeholder mapping, objection handling, and the best entry point for your offer.
C
Prospect Agent here. Give me a company name, a contact, or a deal you are working - I will tell you everything you need to know before the call.
Objection Handler - Paste a prospect's reply and get 3 ready-to-send responses, matched to their objection type and your pipeline stage.
C
Paste the prospect's reply and I will give you 3 ways to respond, matched to their objection type and your pipeline stage.
